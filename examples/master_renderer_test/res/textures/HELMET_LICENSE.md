# DamagedHelmet

`../models/helmet.obj` and `helmet_*.jpg` in this directory are a conversion of
Khronos' glTF sample asset
[DamagedHelmet](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/DamagedHelmet),
made from `glTF-Binary/DamagedHelmet.glb` by `local/temp/glb_to_obj.py` (mesh to
OBJ, the PBR maps kept in their original JPEG encoding). The upstream download
and the conversion are both reproducible from that script.
