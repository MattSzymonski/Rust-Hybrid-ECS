# Pill Engine Privacy Notice

This notice explains how personal data is handled when you contribute to the
Pill Engine Project, sign a contributor agreement, take a Partner License or
contact the Licensor. It is given under articles 13 and 14 of the EU General
Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Pill Engine itself sends no data to the Licensor. A game or application made
with Pill Engine is responsible for any personal data it processes.

## Who is responsible

The controller is Mateusz Szymoński, residing in Poland, contact@pillengine.org
(the **Licensor**). If the Licensor's rights pass to the planned non-profit
foundation, the foundation becomes the controller of the records it receives,
and this notice is updated to name it.

You do not have to provide this data, but contributions are accepted only
under a CLA, and a Partner License needs the details of its parties.

## Who receives it

- **The public.** Your name, email and platform account in commits, pull
  requests and issues are public, and the CLA Assistant comment on your
  pull request shows whether you signed.
- **Service providers** that process it for the Licensor:
  - CLA Assistant (cla-assistant.io, operated by SAP SE, Germany), which
    records CLA acceptances in a database hosted on Microsoft Azure in
    Europe;
  - GitHub, Inc. (USA), which hosts the repository, pull requests, issues
    and the text of the agreements; and
  - OVH SAS, which hosts the contact@pillengine.org mailbox.
- **A successor.** If the Licensor's rights pass to the planned non-profit
  foundation, independent of any company (see
  [`GOVERNANCE.md`](../GOVERNANCE.md)), the CLA and Partner License records
  pass to it too, because they prove the rights it receives.

OVH SAS keeps the data in the European Union, and CLA Assistant stores it
in Europe. GitHub, Inc. is outside the European Economic Area, and transfers
to it rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Where SAP SE transfers data
outside the European Economic Area, it relies on the EU standard contractual
clauses.

## How long it is kept

- CLA records: for as long as Pill Engine contains or distributes your
  Contributions, and then until claims about them are time-barred.
- Commit, issue and review history: permanently, as part of the project's
  history.
- Messages: for as long as needed to handle them and any resulting claims.
- Partner License and invoicing records: for the agreement's term and then
  for the periods required by tax and accounting law.

## Your rights

You may ask for access to your data, its correction or deletion, a
restriction of its use, or a copy in a portable format, and you may object to
processing based on legitimate interest. Write to contact@pillengine.org. The
Licensor may ask you to confirm your identity first.

Some data cannot be deleted while it is still needed: a CLA record proves the
license behind code that is still distributed, and public commit history
cannot be rewritten without harming everyone who relies on it. Where that
applies, you will be told why.

No decisions about you are made by automated means alone.

## Changes

Changes to this notice are published in the Pill Engine Project's repository.
