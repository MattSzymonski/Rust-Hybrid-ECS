Contributor gives the consent described in section 2 of the Individual CLA
by accepting that agreement.

Contributions, or has the owner's permission, and that its Authorized
Contributors identify any third-party material and any parts generated by

in the Pill Engine Project's governance. This agreement does not include a
Partner License.

1.6. By written notice, the Organization may stop this agreement from
covering Contributions made after the notice. Contributions made before it

Organization signed. A new version applies only if the Organization signs

including the Licensor's heirs, who may also complete the transfer described
