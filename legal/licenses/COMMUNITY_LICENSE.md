## 4. Revenue threshold

Anyone may use this license, at any revenue. The threshold decides only
whether you may keep your Pill Engine Modifications private: you may while
your Annual Revenue is **below USD 300,000**, and during the transition
period in section 7. A User with no completed financial year uses its
expected revenue for the current year.

An arrangement made mainly to keep Annual Revenue below the threshold, such
as splitting a business, structuring its ownership or control, or moving
work to another entity, is disregarded, and the revenue of everyone involved
is counted together.

threshold.
