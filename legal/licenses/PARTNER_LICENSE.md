# Pill Engine Partner License - Summary of Terms

## Overview

This page summarizes the Partner License. It is not a license or an offer:
a Partner License exists only as a written agreement signed by the Partner
and the Licensor. Capitalized terms are used as defined in the
[Community License](COMMUNITY_LICENSE.md).

The Partner License is a paid agreement that lets you keep your Pill Engine
Modifications private at any revenue. It is meant mainly for Users whose
Annual Revenue, together with their User Affiliates and from all sources,
is above the Community License's threshold of USD 300,000, where that
license requires them to publish their modifications, but any User may ask
for one.

It is always a choice, never a requirement. Above the threshold you may
instead stay on the Community License and publish your Pill Engine
Modifications, or use Pill Engine under the [LGPLv3](LGPL-3.0_LICENSE.txt)
licensing route. See the main [`LICENSE`](../../LICENSE) file.

Partner funding supports the further development of Pill Engine, which stays
free for everyone: today through the Licensor and, once it exists, through
the planned non-profit foundation, independent of any company (see
[`GOVERNANCE.md`](../GOVERNANCE.md)).

## Rights granted

- You may keep your Pill Engine Modifications private at any revenue,
  including those used in your editors, tools, asset pipelines and servers.
  The publishing duty in section 7 of the Community License does not apply.
- Everything the Community License allows continues to apply.
- The license covers you and the User Affiliates named in the agreement.
- You may keep selling and updating User Projects released during the term
  after it ends, on the terms set in the agreement.
- If agreed, the license may also include warranties, a different
  attribution notice or permission for a Pill Engine Redistribution.

## Limitations

- Third-party components are not covered and stay under their own licenses.
- Only code the Licensor owns or may sublicense under the contributor
  agreements or section 7.3 of the Community License is covered. Before
  signing, the Licensor states in writing which code is covered.
- A Pill Engine Redistribution, such as a fork or a standalone engine, is
  permitted only if the agreement says so, and only within what the
  contributor agreements allow. The same applies to presenting a Pill Engine
  Modification as the official Pill Engine.
- No rights in the Pill Engine name or logos are granted beyond what the
  agreement states.
- The license gives no seat or vote in the Pill Engine Project's governance.

## Indicative pricing

The Partner License is a flat annual fee set by your Annual Revenue. It
takes no share of your revenue and does not grow with the success of a
single User Project.

| Annual Revenue             | Indicative fee                        |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| USD 300,000 to 1,000,000   | USD 1,000 per month, billed annually  |
| USD 1,000,000 to 2,000,000 | USD 2,500 per month, billed annually  |
| USD 2,000,000 to 4,000,000 | USD 6,000 per month, billed annually  |
| USD 4,000,000 to 6,000,000 | USD 10,000 per month, billed annually |
| Above USD 6,000,000        | Set individually                      |

A change of band applies from the next renewal of the agreement.

The first 10 partners get 50% off for 2 years and keep today's prices.

These amounts are an invitation to discuss terms, not an offer. The fee in
a signed agreement is the one that applies, and this pricing may change.
