The names **Pill Engine** and **Pill** (as the short name for Pill Engine)
and the Pill Engine logo are trademarks of Mateusz Szymoński (the
**Licensor**), who uses them to identify the official Pill Engine. The
licenses let you use, change and share the code; they do not give rights in
the names or the logo.

This policy applies whichever license you use Pill Engine under, so
everyone can tell what the official Pill Engine is.

## Permitted uses

- Say truthfully that your game or application is "made with Pill Engine",
  and show the unmodified logo in its credits, splash screens and store
  pages.
- Say that a plugin, tool or asset is "for Pill Engine" or "compatible with
  Pill Engine", as long as its own name does not start with "Pill Engine"
  or "Pill".
- Write, teach and talk about Pill Engine: articles, tutorials, books,
  courses, videos and talks.
- Run non-commercial community groups and events about Pill Engine,
  including their accounts and pages, with a name that shows they are
  community-run, such as "Pill Engine Meetup Paris".
- Say that a fork or other modified version is "based on Pill Engine".

## Uses requiring written permission

- Use "Pill Engine", or a name or logo that could be confused with it, in the
  name of your product, company, service, domain or account.
- Distribute a fork or other modified version of Pill Engine under the name
  "Pill Engine". A fork, including one made under the LGPLv3, must use a
  different name and logo.
- Change the logo, or combine it with your own marks.
- Sell merchandise with the name or logo.
- Suggest that the Licensor or the Pill Engine Project endorses, certifies
  or partners with you, unless you hold a Partner License that says so.

## Use of the name and logo

- Write the name as "Pill Engine" or "Pill", with that spelling and
  capitalization.
- Follow the [brand identity guide](https://docs.pillengine.org/guide/other/brand-identity.html),
  which holds the official logo files and the guidelines for using them.
  Use those files unmodified.
- Make it clear that your product is yours, not the Licensor's.
