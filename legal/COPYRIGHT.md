- Contributors, or the organizations they work for, own the code they
  wrote. Through the [Individual CLA](contributing/CLA.md) or
  [Corporate CLA](contributing/CCLA.md) they license it to the Licensor,
  non-exclusively, for all three licensing routes in the main
  [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE). Nobody assigns copyright.
- A Partner License can cover only code the Licensor owns or holds under a
  CLA or section 7.3 of the
  [Community License](licenses/COMMUNITY_LICENSE.md), never third-party
  components.
- No foundation exists yet. Rights move to one only through its founding
  act or valid written agreements, as described in
  [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md).

## Third-party material

Redistributed third-party files are listed in
[`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) with their license and
source. New dependencies follow the policy in
[`CONTRIBUTING.md`](contributing/CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Concerns

Write to contact@pillengine.org if you believe Pill Engine contains
material that infringes your rights. Justified claims are fixed in the next
release.
