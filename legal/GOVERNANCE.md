# Pill Engine Governance

## Current governance

The **Pill Engine Project** is the open-source effort that develops the
**Pill Engine** software: its repository, community and governance.

- **Mission:** a modern, fast and modular engine for games and other
  real-time applications, whose full source is open to everyone and which is
  free for everyone who builds with it.
- **Licensor:** Mateusz Szymoński leads the Pill Engine Project. They make
  technical and licensing decisions, review and merge contributions, publish
  releases and sign Partner Licenses.
- **Maintainers:** the Licensor may appoint maintainers to review and merge
  contributions in parts of the project.
- **Decisions:** anyone may propose a change or disagree with a decision in a
  public issue in the Pill Engine Project's repository (currently on
  GitHub). Significant changes are discussed in that issue first; the
  Licensor decides and explains the decision in the same issue.
- **Open development:** issues, reviews and design discussion are public,
  except security reports, personal data and confidential commercial terms.
- **Money does not buy control:** sponsorship, Partner Licenses and corporate
  CLAs give no vote, veto or priority in decisions. Paid development work
  goes through normal review and is disclosed on the roadmap. The Licensor
  discloses any paid relationship relevant to a decision.
- **Licensing:** only the Licensor grants the Community License and Partner
  Licenses, and only for rights they hold or received through a CLA or
  section 7.3 of the Community License.
- **Continuity:** licenses already granted, including the LGPLv3 and the
  Community License, continue whatever happens to the Licensor.

## Planned governance

The Licensor intends to found a non-profit foundation for Pill Engine,
independent of any company, and to transfer the Pill Engine Project to it. The
foundation will hold the project's rights, keep Pill Engine available under an
open-source license, keep the Community License free for everyone, and manage
the Partner Licenses whose funding supports development. It is planned to work
as follows:

- **Leadership:** the Licensor, as the foundation's founder, leads its
  Management Board and the project as Project Lead, deciding its technical
  direction and releases, as today. The statute will reserve to the founder
  the appointment of the Management Board, amendments to the statute, and
  consent to changes to Pill Engine's licenses or to transfers of its
  rights, so the project's direction stays steady.
- **No control by money:** no sponsor or Partner License holder may gain
  control of the foundation, or a seat on its Management Board, through its
  funding or agreements; a seat held under the founding act or the statute
  is not.
- **Transparency:** the foundation's income and spending are reported publicly
  each year.
- **Succession:** the statute will say who leads the foundation if the
  Licensor steps down or can no longer act.
- **Existing licenses:** licenses already granted keep their terms; the
  foundation takes the Licensor's place in them.

Until the foundation exists, the Licensor holds these rights. They move to
it only through its founding act or written agreements, in the form the law
requires, using the advance consent in the contributor agreements. The
foundation's statute, once adopted, replaces this document.
